Reading the map

  • The two ports of a station (2k and 2k+1) land on different switches: even ports on st2, st4, st6, odd ports on st1, st3, st5.
  • CT n is AFM slot n-1: CT05 = pod-1-4-ComputeTray. st n is pod-1-(n-1)-SwitchTray, 10.255.4.70+n.
  • The switch port description reads To ComputeTray Slot<s>/GPU<g>[<acc>]/Port<p>, with acc = 4 x slot + GPU. AFM numbers a tray's accelerators differently.
  • The AFM controllers run on st1, st2 and st3.
  • CT05 is down and quarantined since 13 Oct: its 144 switch ports are cabled but have no link.
